# Morning brief

## Steps

1. Read `STATE.md` for what's stuck and what's next.
2. Read today's calendar. List each event with its time and one line on what to prepare.
3. Read email from the last 24 hours. Pick out only what needs a reply from me. For each one:
   who it's from, what they want, and a draft reply (don't send it).
4. Check `inbox.md` for anything dated today or marked urgent.
5. Write the brief, in this order, under one page:
   - **Today:** the calendar, with prep notes
   - **Needs a reply:** each email with its draft
   - **Stuck:** from `STATE.md`, with the one question that would unblock each item
6. Send the brief to me in chat (or wherever I said I read it in `context/me.md`).

## Where it stops

- Drafts are drafts. Nothing is sent to anyone but me without my yes.
- Don't accept, decline or move calendar events. Suggest it instead.

## How you know it worked

- Every event on today's calendar is in the brief.
- Every "needs a reply" item has a draft.
- The brief fits on one screen.
- If a calendar or inbox couldn't be read, the brief says so at the top instead of skipping it.
